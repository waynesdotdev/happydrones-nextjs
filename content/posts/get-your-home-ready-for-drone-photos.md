From above, the camera sees everything at once: the roof, the garden, the drive and the bins. A little preparation goes a long way.

## Before I arrive

- Move cars off the drive and away from the front of the house
- Put bins out of sight, round the side or in the garage
- Mow the lawn a day or two before, so it has time to look fresh
- Tidy away hoses, toys and garden tools
- Open curtains and blinds, and switch on indoor lights if we're filming at dusk

## On the day

You don't need to be home for outdoor shots. If we're doing an FPV fly-through inside, I'll walk the route with you first and agree which doors should be open.

> The best listing photos look effortless, but almost all of them started with someone moving the bins.
